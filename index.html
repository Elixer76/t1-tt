What it is

T1 Traffic Tap is a traffic rotator run by Phillip P. Brewer, who says he has provided traffic online since 2008. You submit one link, and visitors are sent to it every day from a mix of sources.

You do not need an email list or technical skills. If you can paste a link, you can use it. You can swap your link whenever you want, and you get a private stats link so you can see what arrives.

Straight talk before you sign up

  • Traffic is not sales

    Clicks are clicks. A weak page turns more traffic into more people leaving. Send visitors to something with a clear hook and a reason to act.

  • Setup is manual

    The vendor sets up each package by hand. Allow up to 24 hours after you submit your link for the confirmation email and stats link.

  • Your link has limits

    The link must be for an internet marketing, business opportunity, work from home, make money online, or crypto offer. The vendor accepts links at his discretion.

  • Free trial hold

    PayPal places a temporary $1 authorization hold for the free trial. It is meant to drop off your statement within a few days.

  • Keep your link working

    If your link stops working, it is paused for a week and then archived. You can reactivate it later.

  • No promises

    Results depend on your offer, your page, and the work you put in. Nobody can promise you sales.

Who it fits

It suits newer marketers who have no list and no ad budget and want an inexpensive way to put an offer in front of people. It also suits experienced marketers who want one more steady source running in the background.

If your offer is outside the accepted categories, or your page does not convert yet, fix that first.

My advice: do not paste a bare link and hope. Make a short review video, give people value, and warm them up before they land on your offer.
